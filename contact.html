Frequent Enquiries

Q. How do I verify a lot's provenance?

All items undergo a rigorous 3-stage authentication process by our in-house specialists and external forensic partners.

Q. What are the bidding premiums for live auctions?

Our standard buyer's premium is 25% on the first $500,000, and 20% thereafter, exclusive of local taxes.

Q. Do you offer climate-controlled temporary storage?

Yes, we operate high-security bonded warehouses in Geneva, London, and Singapore for up to 90 days post-sale.

Q. How can I register as a phone bidder?

Phone bidding requires pre-registration and a financial reference check completed 48 hours before the auction.

Q. What payment methods are accepted for settlement?

We accept wire transfers in USD, EUR, GBP, and select cryptocurrencies via our institutional partners.
