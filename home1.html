FAQ

Common Questions

How do I register to bid?+
Create a free account, complete identity verification and add a payment method. Registration takes under 5 minutes.
Is buyer authentication guaranteed?+
Yes. Every lot is independently authenticated before listing. All purchases include a certificate of authenticity and are covered by our full money-back guarantee.
What currencies can I bid in?+
We support USD, EUR, GBP, JPY, CHF, AED and 40+ additional currencies. All bids are displayed in your selected currency in real time.
How is secure payment handled?+
Payments are processed through PCI-DSS Level 1 certified gateways. We accept wire transfer, credit card and cryptocurrency for high-value lots.
Can I sell items through OAW?+
Absolutely. Submit your item via our Consign page. Our specialists will provide a complimentary estimate within 5 working days and guide you through the entire process.
What shipping options are available?+
We partner with specialist fine art shippers offering white-glove, fully insured door-to-door delivery worldwide. Local collection from our galleries is also available.
